# Shared scientific prior and implementation record

This package implements the **two frozen cut_v3 alternatives**, `contour_push` / `boundary_graph` and `visual_push` / `dual_view_memory`, for one scene instance and one fixed image placement per invocation. It does not change any cut, mnemonic referent, anchor, exclusion, or policy identity. They are alternatives, not serial stages or an ensemble. The executor, not this package, independently trains each network for 20,000 AdamW updates, seed 0, with the mandated warmup/cosine schedule and .999 EMA. No optimizer, preliminary performance study, checkpoint search, rollout or hardware client is included.

Start with [POLICY_CATALOG.md](POLICY_CATALOG.md), [CALLING.md](CALLING.md), and [HANDOFF.json](HANDOFF.json). Mechanism-specific responsibility, losses, assumptions and limitations are in [contour_push_PRIOR.md](contour_push_PRIOR.md) and [visual_push_PRIOR.md](visual_push_PRIOR.md).

## Family decisions (design choices, not comparisons)

* **Contour:** a five-component diagonal-Gaussian conditional current-command distribution, driven by boundary message passing and masked two-layer gated temporal memory. Diffusion is allowed, but not selected: the calling contract needs one seven-dimensional command, not an open-loop chunk; a compact explicit multimodal likelihood allows re-contact alternatives without a denoising schedule or future action windows. This is not evidence that diffusion would perform worse.
* **Visual:** independently, a five-component diagonal-Gaussian conditional current-command distribution, driven by distinct global/target-detail/wrist/goal image encoders and masked gated temporal memory. The same output family is useful for a different representation: short visual response history conditions ambiguity between approach, pushing and withdrawal. Limited demonstrations and a single-command causal contract favor a modest likelihood model over a diffusion trajectory generator here. This is a separate design decision, not a requirement to share a family or a performance conclusion.

Each model contains its own encoders, memory, mixture and displacement head, initialized from scratch. There are no shared motor weights. Prediction chooses the most probable component's mean, not an average of potentially incompatible modes, a retrieved demonstration, an IK controller, or a scripted pushing rule.

## Actual evidence and API-owned adaptations

Third and wrist images at A0, A4800 and A9014 show brown flat pieces, a light tabletop and a long gray rigid rod, including inner-hole engagement. Metadata confirms calibrated camera blocks and an EE-set TCP, but not rod width, contact or surface clearance. Numerical inspection confirms measured `dq` differs from command `action_json.dq` and initial command nulls exist. No semantic segmentation, character weights, verified contact/success labels or metric table plane is available.

1. **Perception assumption replaced explicitly:** no assets are downloaded. `perception.py` implements automatic broad brown/cardboard chromaticity segmentation, a bright low-saturation support hull, connected components, small-gap filling and forward Hungarian appearance/area/motion association. It requires no human per-move point/mask and contains no letter templates, fonts, class IDs or known-alphabet lookup. It can propose novel silhouettes under its material/background assumptions. It is **not** the source plan's assumed learned generic segmentation/flow model, and its confidence is not calibrated. Generality is reduced to separable brown-like pieces on a light support; different materials, severe occlusion, touching pieces and brown background clutter can fail. This scientific adaptation is shared by both policies, not a third learned policy or a substitute for training either motor model.
2. **Semantic recognition remains external:** inventory IDs are arbitrary scene-scoped tracks, not letters. The HLA/user-target interpretation must determine characters/upright direction, repeated-instance allocation, impossible inventories and layout. No open-alphabet recognizer is claimed trained on these episodes. Geometry-conditioned manipulation of entirely unseen shapes remains a required, **untested** target; new-shape acceptance by the converter is not competence.
3. **Calibrated chart, not metric reconstruction:** RGB remapping uses each episode's calibrated Brown-Conrady block, including calibrated rather than factory wrist K. Coordinates exposed to callers remain the original 1280x720 undistorted third-image chart. Detection runs on a 640x360 raster (one internal pixel = two original chart pixels). Frozen anchor boxes are rasterized in raw original pixels and remapped; they are never applied directly to an undistorted image. Wrist pose is `T_base_flange @ T_flange_cam`. TCP projection uses `T_base_ee` directly; its 0.2-m TCP is not added twice. A finite projected point is not a detected rod silhouette. Projection validity is exposed; visual tip verification and tool clearance are unavailable, not invented. `T_base_board` is never treated as a commissioned tabletop homography. Depth is unused.
4. **Bounded goal renderer:** executable rigid image-plane rotation/translation of the invocation's observed foreground and masked RGB. Clockwise angle is relative to this template, not semantic upright. The caller must acknowledge similarity approximation. Translation beyond 520 original pixels, clipping, workspace violation and malformed goals are refused. Metric/projective layouts require commissioning and a different renderer; this version reports `calibration_required` in the reason rather than pretending to implement them. Shape scale is never arbitrarily warped to relabel an unchanged action. Similarity registration of hindsight masks is heuristic; perspective consistency and physical feasibility are not certified.
5. **Practical compute:** eight observations spaced by three original rows, with mask-padding rather than borrowing another segment. Graphs have at most 256 nodes; variable outer/inner loops retain explicit adjacency. Visual global 320x180, wrist 256x144, target/tool detail 160x160 and foreground/RGB goal 160x90 replace the proposed larger crops. These are implementation resolutions, not inspection-preview constraints. The RGB goal is a masked full-chart target appearance rather than a separate high-resolution tight goal crop; fine goal texture is consequently limited. Cache includes crop-to-original transforms. These reductions can impair small-tool/edge discrimination; no resolution-performance claim is made. The graph has an absolute robot-state readout, not false image/action rotational equivariance.
6. **Candidate versus execution:** lack of controller verification does not block offline learning or candidate computation. It does prevent `decode_action` from producing an enabled command artifact. The decoder requires externally verified recording units, joint order, timing, limits, watchdog/stop, tool/workspace and singularity checks, and rejects rather than clips violations. Even an enabled artifact performs no I/O. `goal_observed` is a geometric stop advisory only, explicitly narrower than the plan's clearance-qualified success-like exit. Its `successor_ready` is always false because clearance, stability/readability of the whole layout, and safe successor travel are not measured here.

## Complete causal data path

`prepare_data` traverses all 22 original `[start,stop)` intervals, 16,745 materialized paired rows, without realignment or a fabricated N+1 row. It decodes each view once per materialized row into cache preparation. Within each segment, all components are detected/tracked **forward before the anchor is used**. The specified anchor box selects an endpoint component and hence a causal track identity. Retrospective identity labeling is permitted; no endpoint image, smoothed mask, label-derived current pose, segment ID, episode index or demonstration order enters live-state features. First valid observed mask of that track is used only for template-to-goal registration validation. The goal is the actual anchor's inferred foreground/masked RGB, not a global endpoint scene or inferred word.

Missing/ambiguous anchor association or similarity-inconsistent shape suppresses the segment's condition-dependent examples and is reported. Current rows need an observed, not carried-forward, selected mask with association/area confidence proxy >= .3 and finite mandatory state. Lost masks are never hallucinated from later frames. Difficult motions, staging, withdrawals and B-R posture/re-contact are not manually removed; automatic exclusions are counted per segment. If these motions fail perception, the coverage record—not an assertion of full learned coverage—is authoritative.

Native action cache is float64, exactly parsed finite `action_json.dq`, never measured `dq`, `v/w`, target_pose or null-to-zero imputation. The unchanged handoff bands remove action loss only; context within that same segment remains permitted. Eligible examples preserve episode ordinal, original index and segment ordinal for audit only. Positive sampling weight is recorded elapsed time (bounded to avoid pathological gaps), with contiguous identical-command run mass capped at 0.5 seconds, normalized to equal mass per segment with eligible examples. This mitigates 30-Hz repetition of 20-Hz teleoperation and long pauses without claiming independent decisions.

The frame cache stores numerical state, selected markers, RGB crops and graph nodes/edges/masks; histories are index maps, not duplicated image sequences. Its size is below the 64-GB limit. `make_batch` only gathers bounded mmap slices and transfers tensors. Goal boxes and public data access exist only in preprocessing. `act` reuses the same camera, detection, tracking, state, crop and graph functions with current observations and caller goals.

71 value channels and 71 finite/missing flags include q, measured dq, tau_ext, the first 3x4 blocks of EE/flange transforms, gripper fields, dt, original signed image ages, repeat indicators, wrist pose, projected TCP validity, selected center/area and association confidence. Invalid gripper -1/NaN is zero-filled only **with a missingness flag**, never interpreted as open/closed. Time differences are robot-clock differences; negative recorded camera age is retained, not repaired. Absolute timestamps, segment progress and sample numbers are not neural features. Normalizers are computed from the common training cache, saved in metadata and model buffers. Commands are normalized only inside loss/head conversion; candidates are denormalized back to recorded-native coordinates.

## Objectives and augmentation

Both models optimize exact mixture negative log likelihood plus 0.15 times a real-gradient smooth-L1 prediction of selected-object displacement three rows later (normalized chart displacement times 100). The auxiliary exists only when both endpoints are eligible in the same original segment; it never becomes an online current feature. There is no contact-affinity ground truth or invented phase/force supervision. Graph attention is trained through action/response loss, not called a calibrated contact predictor.

Training-only bounded photometric gain/bias is consistent across current/goal streams with independent wrist gain; selected masks are not recolored or moved. Nonlatest history observations are dropped with probability .08 while the latest valid frame is retained. No arbitrary rotations, relabeled goals, shape warps, contact labels or action chunks are synthesized. The graph is order-invariant by shared adjacency message passing and pooled readouts, without requiring a stored glyph order.

## Interpretation of results and open requirements

Syntax/interface checking is not a semantic or performance study. Training loss, nonzero gradients, changed weights and checkpoint reload do not establish robot success. There is no held-out set or performance-selected checkpoint in this round. Future evaluation must separate held-out identities **and physical shape families**, novel words/repeated letters, new arrangements/layouts, their joint combination, impossible inventories and ambiguity; also test lighting, occlusion, materials/friction, calibrated tolerances and failures. The same two policies on the same two episodes do not add demonstration diversity. No superiority, collision-free planning, reliable contact, recovery from falls/stacks/flips, arbitrary homing, word success or independent safety redundancy is claimed.
