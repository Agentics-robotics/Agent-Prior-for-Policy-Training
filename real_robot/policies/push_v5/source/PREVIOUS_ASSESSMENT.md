## Training rationale
Do not train this exact provisional cache merely because142 rows and finite gradients pass structural checks. Reviewed retained rows can teach the wrong physical actor extent, boundary type, contact normal and goal registration. Broadening uncertainty without repairing the actor model does not make those labels scientifically sound. The implemented compact identity-blind scorer, masked marginal loss and fixed600-update A-train/B-development schedule remain a starting implementation, not completed learning. A readiness change requires reviewed causal full-instance masks/tracks and contact topology, a validated table/top-surface and physical tool-support conversion (through reliable multi-view annotation or commissioning measurements), and propagation of calibration/visibility/timing uncertainty into both labels and online guards, then a new actual coverage/target audit. Some outer-contact supervision appears recoverable; this blocked decision does not assert all demonstrations are unusable. B has already informed preparation and is only development, never an untouched generalization test. Freeze the exact checked source and its diagnostics as a blocked implementation rather than publishing unreliable weights as a completed policy.
