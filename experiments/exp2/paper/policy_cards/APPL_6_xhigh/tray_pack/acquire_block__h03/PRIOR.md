# Attachment-consequence diffusion prior: acquire_block__h03

## Assigned hypothesis and scope

The original h03 hypothesis is **attachment as an action-conditioned invariant**: a grasped cube co-moves with the TCP under an approximately constant relative transform, while a released cube decouples. The intended benefit is causal discrimination of close-with-object from close-empty, rather than recognition of elapsed manipulation phase. The original proposal combines a learned action-conditioned future model with diffusion-sample ranking, with uncertainty-aware fallback. The source heuristic is not modified by this package; the implementation choices and interface adaptations below are separate.

This is a learned original-action diffusion model, not a task-space controller. It trains on every valid sample in all 24 assigned expanded slices, without cropping to grasp-only frames, resegmentation, elapsed-time inputs, or replay. Thus the same network learns initial empty red approach/descent, closure, lift and initial translation; and incoming red lowering, opening/release, empty retreat, travel to blue, blue descent/closure/lift and initial translation. The explicit independent-object branch matters especially after red release. Red being near its goal is not evidence that it is the current payload.

## Observational evidence and overlap

Actual observations inspected for this implementation include:

- `demo10300`: 0, 75, 100, 119, 120, 130, 160, 181, 265, 290, 300, 310, 360, 414, 445, 479, 480, 500, 520, 540, 560, 561.
- `demo10307`: 100, 110, 130, 150, 165, 259, 287, 310, 439, 477, 487, 505, 530, 540, 551.
- `demo10302`: 78, 273, 302, 454, 495, 575.

For demo10300, red rises from z=0.0208 at 120 to 0.2737 m at 160, with TCP z=0.0207 to 0.2736 m and a roughly 9.5 mm world-x separation. At 181 it is already translating, with object/TCP z about 0.281 m. Blue has an approximately 8.2 mm world-x separation during its lift: z=0.0425 at 500, 0.2132 at 520, and 0.2890 m at 561. These are observations supporting kinematic attachment, not measured forces. Closed finger positions are approximately 0.0183 and 0.0182 m per finger, giving about 0.0365 m total width. Finger velocities oscillate even in these successful grasps and are not used alone as a slip label.

The reverse transition is substantive training, not padding. At demo10300 265, incoming red is at z=0.1121 m, near its goal in xy and moving with the closed TCP. By 290 it is at z=0.0360 m, fingers are opening (about 0.0371 m per finger), and TCP is at z=0.0453 m. At 300/310 red stays settled while fingers finish opening and TCP rises. At 360/414, the empty TCP retreats and travels toward blue. Demo10307 259/287/310 and demo10302 273/302 corroborate this distinction. Consequently, a handoff requesting blue while red is still attached must finish the red transition before attempting blue pickup.

The measured shared ranges include demo10300 [75,182), [265,415), [445,562), with analogous ranges across all 12 demonstrations. They cover early descent, closure and initial motion on pickup, as well as attachment breaking, settling and empty retreat in the reverse transition. Measured starts range from initial TCP z=0.442 m with fully open fingers to incoming red descent with approximately grasp-width fingers. Measured exits have TCP z roughly 0.280--0.293 m and nonzero translation. These ranges are training support, not validated deployment rejection thresholds. All cited indices are inside assigned expanded slices; the exclusive stop index is never cited as a training action.

## Interface adaptations (implemented, not hidden claims)

1. **No candidate-ranking hook exists.** The fixed outer framework runs one 100-step DDPM chain, affine action decoding and controller bounds handling. This package does not sample multiple complete chunks, estimate their likelihoods, replace the sampler, or claim a highest-likelihood fallback. Instead it uses action-conditioned forecast summaries inside every epsilon prediction and distills consequences of low-noise clean-action estimates during training. This is an amortized consequence prior, not mathematically equivalent to multi-sample ranking. Its benefit relative to the original ranking hypothesis remains to be tested.
2. **Only two raw observations are available.** There is no supplied selected-object role, past-action history, requested-object token or executed-command channel. The encoder uses both ordered colors, their goals, measured qpos/qvel, and consecutive TCP/object poses. The learned model can infer nominal context from this geometry, but cannot obey an arbitrary externally changed object request that is observationally indistinguishable. Expert command values are used only in training future-regime labels; noisy proposed actions are candidate inputs, not a fabricated record of executed commands. The inference API should retain selected identity and longer history for its own handoff reasoning when available.
3. **No measured attachment or force labels exist.** Conservative kinematic proxy labels supervise attachment. Ambiguous contact/closure frames are excluded from regime/rigidity supervision while their action and future-pose targets remain in training. Uncertainty means a learned forecast residual scale and regime ambiguity, not a calibrated miss probability, safety guarantee or counterfactual outcome model.

## Representation and learned modules

Inputs remain `[B,2,47]` raw causal observations. Actions remain `[B,16,8]` affinely encoded seven absolute Panda joint targets and gripper command, increasing toward open. The public shared normalizer is used without any refit. `std` is the supplied half range, not an empirical per-skill standard deviation. World relative-position features use the complete-demonstration TCP xyz half ranges; TCP-local relative positions use their maximum as an isotropic scale. Quaternion components retain unit bounds. Physical label tolerances and residual parameterizations below are not replacement normalizers.

The 167-dimensional causal feature vector contains 94 normalized raw-history components; for each observation, two TCP-local relative translations/quaternions, both goal-minus-object and goal-minus-TCP vectors and the blue-minus-red displacement (29 per observation); plus TCP/red/blue displacement differences and local-relative displacement differences across the two observations (15). These are displacements, not velocities computed with an invented timestep. Quaternion features use a dominant-component sign convention, while all quaternion losses are sign-invariant. There is no image encoder, FK provider or IK module.

A 256-wide MLP and LayerNorm encode history. A trainable head predicts exclusive `none/red/blue` attachment logits. The three-way categorical distribution disallows assigning probability one to both blocks simultaneously; it does not verify real attachment. Color ordering and raw world/joint features are retained. TCP-local geometry does not make world goals or joint-target actions equivariant.

A learned forecast consumes the history encoding, current belief logits, the entire encoded candidate chunk, slot embeddings and a sinusoidal noise-level embedding. Three 192-wide residual temporal convolution blocks with dilations 1,2,4 model action-chunk consequences. Seeing all candidate actions is permitted: they are proposals, not future observations. At clean training input the level is zero; during DDPM it is `(timestep+1)/100`, so high-noise forecasting is necessarily a conditional expectation rather than known action execution.

The forecast predicts for every slot:

- A TCP translation residual and normalized quaternion residual relative to the latest observed TCP.
- An independent translation/quaternion branch for each color relative to that object's current pose. This is learned support motion, not a hard-coded fixed support plane.
- A learned TCP-to-object transform C for each color, relative to the currently observed transform. The attached branch composes predicted TCP pose with C using quaternion rotation and multiplication.
- Three exclusive future regime logits, initialized through the current belief logits and modified by the learned rollout.
- Three bounded residual-scale predictions, `0.005 + 0.25*sigmoid(output)`, in common normalized position units.

Object pose predictions blend attached and independent branches with their attachment probabilities; quaternion signs are aligned before normalizing the blend. This is a differentiable mixture, not a hard physical constraint. Transform C remains trainable and can change at attachment transitions. A 128-wide encoder embeds the 16-slot forecast summaries: normalized predicted TCP/red/blue displacements, three regime probabilities and three residual scales. Concatenated with history and current belief probabilities, it forms a 387-dimensional condition for the standard public Conditional U-Net, widths 128/256/512, time embedding 128, kernel 5 and groups 8. Thus the structured action-conditioned module is actually used at deployment, rather than being an unused training head.

## Training targets, masks and gradient paths

Action slot j corresponds to action t-1+j and its future label is the state after that action. Forecasts are anchored to the latest causal observation t; slot zero is therefore supervised toward t, not shifted to t+1. Future predecessor labels use observation t-1 at slot zero and the preceding future slot thereafter. All future terms use the intersection of action and future masks; pairwise losses require two valid adjacent slots. Segment padding never becomes a cross-slice training target. Nothing from `future_obs`, expert commands, labels or masks is passed to deployment forward.

Training-only regime proxies use both object identities. A positive label requires total measured finger width in (0.025,0.050) m, near-TCP separation below 0.035 m, TCP-local relative translation change below 0.0025 m, quaternion `1-dot^2` change below 0.001, and matching object/TCP displacement within 0.0025 m. It also requires object displacement above 0.00025 m per observation or elevation above local support by 0.010 m. Current and previous object heights must exceed the estimated support by 0.003/0.001 m to exclude contact/impact from rigidity supervision. Support for this *label only* is goal z when within 0.08 m in goal xy, otherwise table center height 0.020 m. Future attachment additionally requires negative expert command below -0.25. Unique supported attachment labels red or blue. Confident none labels come from opening (width above 0.058 m, or positive expert command above 0.25 and width above 0.046 m) or both objects farther than 0.065 m. Ambiguous close-at-table frames are not labeled as failed grasps. Current-belief labels use the same causal geometry but cannot use commands. These thresholds are nominal scientific assumptions, not validated inference gates.

Let P be robust multistep pose error: SmoothL1(beta=0.05) on xyz error divided by shared TCP xyz scales, averaged over coordinates/entities/valid slots, plus 0.1 times sign-invariant quaternion error `1-dot^2`. Total loss is:

`L = L_epsilon + 0.60 P_clean + 0.20 P_noisy + 0.20 L_branches + 0.08 L_regimes + 0.15 L_rigid + 0.05 L_stationary + 0.05 L_uncertainty + 0.15 L_consequence`.

- **Epsilon** is masked public epsilon MSE on the original encoded actions. It trains the denoiser, history encoder, belief head, forecast and forecast-condition encoder.
- **Clean/noisy pose** supervises learned forecasts with actual future TCP and both object poses, for expert actions at zero noise and for the noisy action candidate. Noisy pose error is weighted by `0.2+0.8*alpha_bar`. Both train forecasts and shared history representations. TCP supervision and actual lift/transport labels prevent a universal immobility solution.
- **Branch pose** uses supported labels to supervise the attached branch for attached objects and the independent branch otherwise. It trains useful alternatives even if the mixture has not yet learned confident gates. It uses the same pose error as P.
- **Regimes** is clean forecast cross entropy plus 0.5 noisy forecast cross entropy plus current causal-belief cross entropy. Opening creates supported detach-to-none labels, not a gripper-command-only attachment rule.
- **Rigidity** penalizes adjacent predicted mixture TCP-relative transform drift and predicted branch-C drift, only when the same object has supported attachment at both times. It is clean rigidity plus 0.5 noisy rigidity. Translation uses the maximum shared position scale and quaternion error coefficient 0.1. These penalties depend on trainable predictions; no observed-pose-only term is presented as a training loss.
- **Stationarity** penalizes predicted object displacement only on adjacent valid independent labels whose actual displacement is below 0.0003 m. It preserves settled red without forcing held red or settling/contact motion to freeze. It is a learned prediction penalty, not a deployment constraint.
- **Uncertainty** fits predicted residual scales to detached normalized position RMSE of each forecast, bounded to [0.005,0.255], with SmoothL1(beta=0.02), for clean and noisy forecasts. Detaching this target prevents worsening a pose estimate simply to fit its own uncertainty. This is residual calibration on nominal data, not epistemic uncertainty.
- **Consequence** reconstructs `x0=(x_t-sqrt(1-alpha_bar)*epsilon)/sqrt(alpha_bar)` and bounds this *training estimate only* to [-1.25,1.25]. It runs x0 through the clean forecast with detached forecast parameters, buffers, context and belief. Predicted TCP and object pose errors are weighted by `alpha_bar^2 * 1[alpha_bar>0.2]` and by detached per-entity confidence `clamp(0.03/(sigma_clean+0.01),0.15,1)`. Gradients flow through forecast action inputs into epsilon, not into the frozen surrogate on this path. This transmits expert lifting, placement, settling and transport consequences to denoising while limiting high-noise amplification and uncertain-model influence. There is no arbitrary height-maximization reward that could prefer lifting the already settled red.

No mask drops an action from the diffusion objective merely because the grasp is ambiguous. No hard force, collision or unilateral-contact constraint is claimed. All coefficients are predeclared, not selected from rollout performance.

## Causal handoff and usage

Use this policy over the full acquisition/transition slice, including partway through shared motion. At initial entry, TCP height, open fingers and both blocks on the table describe an empty approach. At incoming placement, red's near-TCP transform, closed measured fingers, descending co-motion and goal-relative location explain why finishing red release precedes going to blue. At subsequent blue entry, settled red near z=0.036 m and independent of TCP is context to preserve, while blue near table z=0.020 m is the next nominal object. The architecture retains both identities throughout; it does not attach whichever object merely satisfies a goal predicate.

For exit, use causal evidence of elevation *and* consistent relative pose/co-motion with measured grasp-width fingers, plus the actual command history if the inference agent has it. Nominal commands during lift are -1. Endpoints are already translating, so do not demand zero qvel, an exact arm posture or a fixed dwell. A successor should receive selected identity/goal, qpos/qvel, both poses and goals, enough recent TCP/object/finger history to re-estimate attachment, and uncertainty if transfer occurs before co-motion is established. The stock forward returns only epsilon; it does not automatically export a validated readiness flag or telemetry packet. The API must use available observations for that decision. `HANDOFF.json` gives the observed support and limitations.

Closed fingers while the object stays at table height, TCP rising alone, abrupt relative-pose drift, or red rising on an intended empty retreat are failure signatures, not cases with demonstrated robust recovery. Continue the shared close/lift or release/retreat phase when nominal evidence is incomplete; transfer to a successor supporting that overlap with uncertainty rather than falsely declaring pickup complete. Do not reject solely because finger qvel oscillates.

The full task contract remains red_at_goal AND blue_at_goal, with rotated-xy containment and z tolerance as supplied. Gripper release, TCP clearance, slow velocity and sustained stability are not extra task-success requirements. Skill handoff advice must not redefine that contract.

## Budget, dependencies and limitations

Uses only Torch, math and the public numerical backbone/helpers. Fixed budget: seed 0, 20,000 updates, batch 128, AdamW 1e-4/1e-6, cosine schedule with 500 warmup updates, gradient norm 1, EMA 0.999; horizon/history/execution 16/2/8; 100 training and inference DDPM steps, epsilon prediction, clip_sample true. Selection is the last EMA, not best measured performance. All common normalization remains fitted on complete original demonstrations. No source trajectory, table of actions or file access is embedded in the model.

Reliable pose tracking, comparable cube geometry and nominal parallel-jaw dynamics are assumed. Two frames cannot prove sustained attachment or distinguish every static close-empty ambiguity. Learned residual uncertainty can be overconfident on unseen misses, collisions or slip. The mixture and approximate support labels are not a physics simulator. No claims are made about generalized blue-first requests, force inference, collision-free motion, slip recovery, calibrated safety thresholds, rotational/translational action invariance, or successful final task performance. Relevant unrun ablations are the same encoder without rigidity/consequence loss and an unstructured future model without the attached/independent decomposition. The original ranking-specific hypothesis is not directly tested by this adapted interface implementation.
