# Assembly initial design rationale, version 1

This is the actual pre-implementation proposal from `/root/design_assembly` in `codex_session` mode. I inspected only the design contract and the assembly D2 evidence bundle, including all 16 original frames through `view_image`. Both NPZ trajectories were summarized through Pixi. Exact model/session identifiers, token counts and cost are unavailable; the JSON does not invent them. No expert or reward source, larger demonstration set, old score, other task report, or concrete development/test state was opened.

The decisive observed distinction is between the nut handle site and the ring center: they remain approximately 0.130 m apart in both demonstrations. The handle is grasped, the ring rises to approximately 0.20 m, then it moves over the vertical peg and descends. This motivates separate hand-to-handle and ring-to-goal features. Two successful LL/HH sequences do not establish what happens with independent factor combinations or failed grasps.

- **P1: explicit world relations.** Supply point differences, horizontal/vertical alignment and motion alongside world positions to one diffusion policy. This tests whether exposing the grasp and insertion relationships helps factor recombination without imposing a controller or event schedule. Absolute positions remain because the robot and gravity are fixed.
- **P2: transport-aligned action coordinates.** Keep world z, align the horizontal action basis with the current ring-to-goal displacement, and retain world anchors in observation conditioning. Use the same current basis for both history entries and the entire chunk, then decode before world action clipping. The basis falls back to world x inside a 0.020 m radius. This is an invertible coordinate representation, not physical augmentation; the branch near alignment and fixed robot reach are explicit risks.
- **P3: relations plus learned event and future-geometry supervision.** Reuse P1 world features/actions and add small jointly trained heads. Four retrospective event classes softly condition the shared diffusion model through predicted probabilities at both training and runtime. A second head predicts hand/handle and ring/goal relations at 4 and 16 steps. Fixed label rules, masks, weights and ramps are specified in the JSON. The rules produce event starts 22/45/73 and 25/48/78 in the allowed demonstrations; this is a label check, not performance validation.

I adopted the P1 plus learned-supervision combination as P3 and rejected adding P2's moving frame to it in the initial version. Combining a coordinate branch with retrospective event learning would add two poorly supported sources of instability at insertion. These candidates are meaningfully distinct but are not a complete factorial ablation.

All candidates learn every action through diffusion, retain the common numerical runtime schema, use the same total 20,000-update and batch-128 budget, and train every N independently from its own nested D_N. No visual policy, analytic action controller, success oracle, runtime future labels, corrective controller, test action search or geometric data augmentation is proposed. Auxiliary labels are confined to current-D_N training. Parameter figures are prospective estimates until implementation counts them; the hard cap is three times measured B0.

No implementation, training or evaluation was performed by this design agent. The proposal records qualitative expected benefits and failure conditions without assuming priors improve performance. Version 1 must remain preserved before any implementation; a permitted later revision requires its own record.
