# finish_red__h01: rotated-footprint margin-aware diffusion

## Assigned hypothesis and scope

The assigned heuristic statement is: **"Optimize the actual rotated-footprint completion margins, not just object-center proximity or gripper release. Hypothesis: contract-aware future-outcome guidance improves placement reliability and avoids false completion while retaining demonstrated terminal release and retreat."** The source heuristic is unchanged. This document describes implementation choices, not a replacement goal contract.

The policy learns the FULL expanded `finish_red` data, including unfinished blue placement. Source action slices are [600,1064) in demo10100, [605,1064) in demo10101, [602,1060) in demo10102, [606,1065) in demo10103, [598,1057) in demo10104, [599,1056) in demo10105, [601,1059) in demo10106, [613,1071) in demo10107, [600,1058) in demo10108, [604,1063) in demo10109, [607,1064) in demo10110 and [603,1061) in demo10111. Terminal observations at the stop indices supply labels, not actions. No phase is removed, relabeled as a different skill, or selected by a scripted time schedule. All valid actions, including airborne transit, receive ordinary diffusion supervision.

## Executable adaptation of the research proposal

The research proposal suggests a future-pose model and soft online ranking or sampling guidance. The fixed execution interface exposes epsilon prediction, not a custom candidate-selector hook. This implementation deliberately preserves the supplied single-chain, 100-step DDPM sampler. It uses **training-time differentiable outcome guidance**, amortized into the denoiser, together with exact causal margin features and a learned causal placement-mode conditioner. It does NOT rank multiple sampled chunks, run an inference-time gradient optimizer, modify the DDPM schedule, or report those unimplemented mechanisms as implemented. This tests the contract-aware objective part of the hypothesis, not a claim that online ranking and amortized guidance are equivalent.

A supervised, action-conditioned surrogate predicts both objects' future position, unit orientation and height-support proxy over the 16 action slots. The negative predicted-margin objective is differentiated through a frozen copy of that surrogate's parameter tensors into the reconstructed clean action chunk, then into epsilon prediction. Thus the containment penalty genuinely changes the learned action model; it is not a constant penalty on observed poses. Observations determine geometric conditioning and label windows only. Predictor supervision limits, but cannot eliminate, exploitation of a learned surrogate.

## Causal representation and learned architecture

Inputs are exactly two causal 47-dimensional world-frame observations: qpos including two finger positions, qvel, TCP xyz/wxyz, red xyz/wxyz, blue xyz/wxyz, both goal positions and the zero drawer compatibility channels. No images, trajectory identifiers, source indices, future observations or external contact sensors are input to deployment forward.

The complete-demonstration shared range normalizer is used unchanged. Its supplied `std` entries are half-ranges, not empirical standard deviations. Quaternion components keep the shared unit bounds; constant channels keep unit scale. No tiny per-skill scale is fitted. The 47 normalized channels in each state are augmented by 22 exact geometry channels: two object-minus-goal vectors (6), two TCP-minus-object vectors (6), projected XY half-extents (4), and three completion margins for each object (6). Relative vectors use the axiswise maximum of the supplied TCP, red-position and blue-position scales. Half-extents are divided by the 0.06 m region half-width, and margins by [0.06,0.06,0.011] m. These last constants nondimensionalize the known contract and are not fitted normalization statistics.

The two 69-channel states and their difference produce 207 inputs to a 207-256-256 SiLU encoder. Their difference is a sample-to-sample change, not a velocity estimate in seconds; no observation period is assumed. A 256-128-2 SiLU head predicts per-object near-placement/support probabilities from this causal context. Their sigmoid values and the 256-dimensional context form a 258-dimensional U-Net condition. The standard Conditional U-Net has widths 128/256/512, time embedding 128, kernel 5 and 8 groups. It predicts epsilon for shared-normalized **absolute seven joint targets plus gripper command**. It is not an equivariant action representation or a Cartesian controller.

The outcome head encodes the masked candidate action chunk and its validity mask with widths 192/128, concatenates the context, and uses widths 256/256 to predict 16 x 2 x 8 outputs. For each object and slot these are three residual position coordinates scaled by that object's supplied global position scales, four quaternion residuals added to the current unit quaternion and renormalized, and one support logit. The output layer starts with small nonzero weights. The known current pose is a residual baseline, not a prerecorded future. The full proposed chunk may condition each outcome; the head is a local whole-chunk surrogate, not a causally rolled-out physics model. It has no privileged future-state input.

## Exact geometry

For unit wxyz quaternion q, form the standard rotation matrix R. The half-extents of the rotated cube's XY projection are

- e_x = 0.02 sum_j |R_xj|,
- e_y = 0.02 sum_j |R_yj|.

The margins are m_x = 0.06 - |p_x-g_x| - e_x, m_y = 0.06 - |p_y-g_y| - e_y, and m_z = 0.011 - |p_z-g_z|. All three nonnegative margins are exactly the supplied per-object completion geometry. They account for all three columns of each row, not just planar yaw or an unrotated square. Quaternion sign does not affect them. Unit normalization guards invalid near-zero inputs with an identity fallback; data are expected to contain valid orientations.

`observed_contract(raw)` and `model.observed_contract(raw_history)` expose observed red, blue and conjunction booleans without using predictions. The external evaluator remains authoritative; these helpers do not stop, hold or otherwise modify actions. No extra release, velocity, TCP-clearance or sustained-hold predicate is introduced.

## Training objectives and gradient paths

The frozen recipe remains seed 0, 20,000 updates, batch 128, horizon 16, history 2, execution 8, 100 train and inference DDPM steps, epsilon prediction, external sample clipping, AdamW 1e-4 with weight decay 1e-6, cosine schedule with 500 optimizer warmup updates, gradient clipping 1 and EMA .999. Selection is the last EMA at the declared budget, not feedback-based model selection.

1. **Diffusion:** masked epsilon MSE for every valid action slot. This is always the main loss. The U-Net receives the same causal condition in training and deployment.
2. **Pose supervision:** predict outcomes from the demonstrated encoded action chunk. Position error is Smooth L1 divided by the supplied per-object shared position scales. Orientation error is the smaller of squared quaternion distances to q and -q, weighted by 0.25 within pose loss. No empirical angular scale is fitted.
3. **Support supervision:** BCE for each predicted logit against |future p_z - goal_z| <= 0.011 m. This is explicitly a height-based support proxy, NOT true contact or release detection. It can be positive while a block is still gripped.
4. **Causal mode supervision:** BCE on the causal two-logit head. Each label is the fraction of valid future slots with nonnegative rotated XY margins and height error at most 0.05 m. This teaches a local-horizon near-placement/support propensity from current history; it is not an exact success probability and does not hard-switch controllers. It affects epsilon through the learned conditioner even at inference.
5. **Denoised-outcome supervision:** x0 is reconstructed using the batch's actual alpha_bar, x0=(x_t-sqrt(1-alpha_bar)*epsilon)/sqrt(alpha_bar). For auxiliary computations only, x0 is clamped to [-1.25,1.25]. The surrogate is also trained on detached x0 against the observed future labels, with alpha_bar squared reliability. This branch trains the surrogate and shared observation representation, not actions through x0.
6. **Contract margin guidance:** a second x0 outcome evaluation uses detached outcome weights AND detached observation context. Its input x0 is NOT detached. For each object, labels activate the penalty only at future slots whose exact observed margins are all nonnegative. The differentiable penalty is Smooth L1 of ReLU(-predicted_margin/[0.06,0.06,0.011]) to zero, averaged over xyz. There is no pressure to force necessary airborne transit into a support-height target. Predicted support/mode logits do not gate this training loss, so the network cannot evade it merely by declaring transit.
7. **Blue preservation:** when blue is already observed to satisfy its goal, and the valid future label retains blue there, an additional predicted blue-margin penalty and shared-scale Smooth L1 position drift from its current position discourage disrupting blue during red retrieval, transfer and placement. Red placement margins are optimized concurrently when its own placement label window becomes active. This is not a hard collision or preservation guarantee.

All future objectives use `future_mask * mask`; slot zero is excluded because its post-action state (after action t-1) is already the latest causal observation. Slots 1 through 15 predict after actions t through t+14. Masked objectives use weighted means with denominator at least one. Reliability multiplies the numerator but is not canceled by renormalization. Future goals are training labels in these losses; at deployment the same world goals are available causally in the observations. No future labels enter `forward`.

The total prior loss is 0.10 clean_pose + 0.025 denoised_pose + 0.02 clean_support + 0.005 denoised_support + 0.02 causal_mode + ramp*(0.05 margin + 0.025 preservation). Denoised pose/support, margin and preservation are weighted by alpha_bar squared to suppress high-noise amplification. The surrogate is trained before trusting its action Jacobian: ramp is zero for the first 500 loss updates, then rises linearly to one at update 2000. This saved optimization counter is NOT an execution-phase schedule. At the two-update interface check the geometric guidance terms are computed but their optimization weight is still zero; learned auxiliary supervision and the main diffusion loss are active. Guidance thereafter flows only through x0 into the epsilon network (and its causal encoder/mode condition), rather than reducing the hinge by moving the surrogate itself. Supervised pose/support losses train the surrogate separately.

## Applicability, limitations and accounting

This is a learned action diffusion policy, with Torch modules and the supplied `appl.public` backbone/loss. It uses no file access at runtime, simulator, external IK/FK, image encoder, scripted action sequence, trajectory replay, analytic joint controller or extra sampler. Native decoding, DDPM clipping and safe actuator execution are delegated to the repository. The outcome head is not used to claim hard constraints or to override actual observations.

The geometry formula is exact for the supplied cube and square contract, but learned responses to substantial rotations, edge placements, slips, collisions and disturbed blue poses are not established by these almost centered, near-identity successful demonstrations. An outcome head may learn state correlations more strongly than counterfactual action effects; freezing its parameters in the guidance branch prevents direct objective cheating but does not validate its Jacobian off-manifold. The trust region, small coefficients, reliability weighting and warmup are conservative design choices, not guarantees. The horizon only supports local outcome forecasts, not eventual task-success prediction during long transit. Soft mode labels are learned propensities, not calibrated contact beliefs. A contract-satisfying state may still be before demonstrated release/retreat; decisions about continued invocation belong to the inference API. Failed grasp recovery and indefinite terminal holding are unspecified outside training support.

The interface check validates two real training updates, DDPM sampling and EMA reload. It is not a success-rate experiment. Equal-budget comparisons with center-only objectives, unguided DP and online full-footprint guidance remain future scientific tests; this package makes no unmeasured performance claim.
